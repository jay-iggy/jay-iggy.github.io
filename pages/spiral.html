About

Spiral is a bullet hell boss rush game. A strange creature in an old lady's basement has been mutated by a wizard's spell. Face off against a continuously changing and evolving monster as you find yourself way in over your head.

Boss Implementation

I'd like the showcase my programming and animation process I utilized for some of the bosses I created for the game.

Gunther is a giant shotgun that has come to life and crawls around like an inchworm while littering the arena with explosive shotgun shells.
